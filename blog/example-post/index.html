What a post looks like

24 August 2026

This is a layout sample, not something I actually published. It is here so the listing, type, and article width can be checked before a real post goes up.

A post should read like a note I would send a colleague: short, specific, and in my own voice. What I am working on, a thought that will not leave me alone, or both.

What belongs here

  • What I am doing, while it is still in motion
  • Thoughts that are not a paper, and should not be forced into one
  • The occasional update that does not fit Publications, Projects, or CV
Papers stay on Publications. This tab is for the rest of the thinking.
